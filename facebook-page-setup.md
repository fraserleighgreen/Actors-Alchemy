## Brand-alignment checks

Before publishing anything, ask:

1. Is this clearly for performers or organisations training performers?
2. Is the outcome practical and specific rather than vague inspiration?
3. Does the language sound supportive without promising bookings or guaranteed success?
4. Does it connect craft, confidence and professional readiness?
5. Does the design use the existing Actors Alchemy assets and palette rather than a newly invented style?

Avoid describing Actors Alchemy as a casting service, agency, management company, therapy practice or guaranteed route to employment.
