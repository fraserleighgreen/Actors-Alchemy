# Actors Alchemy Agency Taster Strategy

## Purpose

Offer all 20 agency performers a complimentary 20-minute introduction to Actors Alchemy. The aim is to demonstrate Fraser's approach, create one useful shift in the work, gather structured feedback and measure interest in continued paid coaching.

This is a coaching taster, not a free replacement for the 60-minute paid service.

## Scale

- 20 performers
- 20 minutes of coaching per performer
- 400 coaching minutes in total (6 hours 40 minutes)
- Book as 25-minute diary slots to provide five minutes for notes and reset
- Total scheduled time: approximately 8 hours 20 minutes
- Split delivery across two days or four shorter blocks where possible

## Performer preparation

Each performer should bring one short piece of material or one specific question. Suitable focuses include a song cut, a short scene, a character choice, an audition question, a self-tape moment or a mindset challenge.

## Session structure

### Minutes 0-3: The question

Ask what the performer is working on and what currently feels least clear.

### Minutes 3-16: The work

Coach one specific moment. Possible areas include the thought beneath a lyric, a clearer action, character relationship, stakes, given circumstances, where the thought changes, connection with the receiver, or turning nerves into usable focus.

### Minutes 16-20: The takeaway

Summarise what changed, why it changed, what to continue exploring and which form of support may suit the performer.

Suggested closing language:

> This is one place we could take the work. In a full session, or across a longer period, we would have time to explore what sits beneath it and help you make the discovery repeatable.

## Positioning

Preferred description: **Complimentary 20-minute coaching taster**.

Supporting copy:

> Each performer should bring one short piece of material or one specific question. The session offers a focused introduction to Actors Alchemy rather than full audition preparation.

Avoid describing it simply as a free session, because it is not equivalent to the paid 60-minute experience.

## Feedback questions

1. Did you experience a useful shift during the session?
2. Do you feel clearer about the material?
3. What was most valuable?
4. Would you book a full session?
5. Which paid option would you realistically consider?
6. What, if anything, would prevent you from booking?

Suggested barriers to track: price, timing, uncertainty about need, preference for group training, an existing coach, or another reason.

## Follow-up

Do not use the coaching time for a hard sales pitch. Send a short follow-up presenting the paid coaching options, their intended outcomes, total prices and payment options.

## Measures of success

- Useful-shift and clarity ratings
- Repeated language performers use to describe the value
- Interest in individual versus continuous coaching
- Package preferences
- Enquiries and paid bookings within 30 days
- Attendance, cancellations and preparation levels
- Agency engagement and subsequent referrals

Twenty tasters can test the proposition, pricing interest and immediate coaching value. They cannot yet establish the ideal long-term programme length; that should be assessed through completion and outcome data from paid clients.
